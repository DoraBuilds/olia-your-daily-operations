## Typography

- **Cormorant Garamond** (600 / italic) — display & the "Olia" wordmark
- **Hanken Grotesk** (400–700) — interface & body
- **IBM Plex Mono** (400 / 500) — labels, timestamps, data

All three are free on Google Fonts.

---

## Logo rules

- The full-colour gradient mark is for **black / near-black only**. Everywhere else use a single-colour cut (ink, ivory, or champagne).
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to its inner-ring radius.
- Minimum sizes: 48px in UI, 32px favicon (use the app-icon mark below these).
- Don't stretch, recolour with off-brand gradients, or place on busy backgrounds.
